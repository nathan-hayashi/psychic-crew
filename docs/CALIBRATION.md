# CALIBRATION — the standing confidence-vs-outcome ledger (v1, 2026-09-04)

**Purpose.** The estate's predictions, graded. Since F0, agents have carried a 0.6 confidence
threshold that no mechanism ever calibrated — this ledger is where predictions meet their
outcomes, so that over time the threshold's worth is a measured fact, not an inherited
number. It closes the last of the vector program's three motivating holes.

**The oracle, designed not assumed.** A prediction enters WHEN MADE, before the work — the
questions-before-reading law applied to confidence (its row cites the record where the
prediction lives). The outcome enters at the resolving gate, by dated append, citing the
durable artifact that settles it (a map outcome column, a gate close, a released FINDINGS
packet). Nothing here is recalled; every cell points somewhere a reader can re-derive.

**Intake lanes.** (1) Research-yield predictions (the seed six): source-map rows, recomputed
mechanically against the map every run. (2) FALLBACK confidences: a specialist's FALLBACK
block confidence may register here with its task_id; the outcome appends when the task's
resolution lands in a trail or ledger row, cited. (3) Any future gate's pre-declared
expectation, at that gate's discretion. Band vocabularies are declared per lane; the seed
lane's is TAKE-ZERO=0 · TAKE-FEW=1-2 · TAKE-MANY>=3, locked at SOURCE-MAP-1 before any
reading.

## Weakest claims, flagged

`[I]` The seed lane's recomputation is mechanical; the FALLBACK lane's oracle depends on a
resolution row EXISTING — a task abandoned without a durable resolution never grades, and
that absence is visible (outcome stays '-'), not silent. `[E-limits]` Six entries grade one
author in one program; the table earns meaning by accumulation, not by its start.
