# COMM-HARDEN-1, explained plainly

## What changed

Four one-line security tightenings, batched under one operator word. The user-global
any-host fetch allowance is deleted (the zero-network law's one mechanical hole, closed
machine-wide). The generic gh-repo grant narrowed to exactly the two estate-scoped forms
ever used (view and edit, nathan-hayashi only). The turbo repo's upstream remote to the
external original is removed — the machine now has zero remotes outside the operator's
account, making the estate-only communications ruling mechanical, not procedural. And the
agent-teams flag is pinned to 0 at the project layer, where it beats shell exports: the
peer-to-peer surface AT-1 bounded can no longer open by accident here.

## What is bound, and what is LIVE-lined

The two in-repo surfaces carry standing arms with mutated-copy probes. The user-settings
deletion and the remote removal are machine-local acts outside this repo's honest reach —
they live as LIVE lines in the gate's ledger entry (before/after, command and output), per
the no-cross-repo-assertion law.

## What could break, and what catches it

A re-widened grant or a flipped pin — the arms, probe-proven every run. The two
machine-local surfaces regressing — the next COMM-AUDIT-class sweep; their registers stay
resolved with this gate named.
