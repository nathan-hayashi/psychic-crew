# DIVE-W1-DRY, explained plainly

## What this gate is

The wave's close, now earned: the last two dives performed both pulled out nothing (the
frozen rule's condition, met at five completed dives). The token flips every remaining
QUEUED row to PARKED-DRY — questions intact, reopenable — and every unfilled drop request
to UNFILLED-AT-DRY, standing recorded. Nothing is silently dropped; the dated derivation
lands in the map itself.

## The alternative

Any remaining dive token continues the wave instead — dry is a state, not a wall. The
eleven remaining rows: HC-1, OU-1, CG-1, and the eight drop-requests.

## What could break, and what catches it

Three new arms: the dry re-derivation (a DRY section written while the tail is not 0,0
reds unless it carries the operator-early-close line verbatim), terminal-status legality,
and row-count conservation.
