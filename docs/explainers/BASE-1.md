# BASE-1, explained plainly

## What changed

Arc 3 opened by fixing the measuring stick before measuring: the four remaining baseline
candidates (Dify, Langflow, MetaGPT, AutoGen) are named with their documented licenses and
comparison value, the five STRESS-1 axes are reaffirmed verbatim under their frozen law
("verifiable outcome, not velocity"), CrewAI's finished comparison is declared REUSED so its
row stays comparable, and the posture is stated plainly: everything is documented-only —
nothing is on disk and nothing may be fetched — which is why BASE-2's verdict vocabulary
carries NOT-COMPARABLE-DOCUMENTED-ONLY.

## Why

C-18 is the lesson that axes chosen after the data are chosen by the data. Freezing first
costs one small gate and buys a comparison nobody can accuse of fitting its ruler to its
conclusion.
