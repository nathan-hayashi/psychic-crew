# STUB-1, explained plainly

## What changed

The estate's skill surface was audited for the rot class that killed the retired guide —
pasted command output that ages into confident falsehood — and the honest verdict is NO-OP:
all three parent skills are prose-first and transcript-free, the plugins skills are already
held to the stricter zero-CLI law, lite's pack is templates and standards, and three siblings
carry no skill surface at all (recorded, not skipped). What remains is the mechanism: the
audit table is suite-bound both ways against the TRACKED census (never a filesystem glob —
the reference corpora carry ~30 third-party skill files a glob would sweep in), and a narrow
ratchet holds the surface at zero pasted-terminal shapes forever, fire-probed every run.

## Why a NO-OP gate is worth a token

Because the alternative is a claim nobody checked. The audit's value is the evidence and the
standing ratchet, not invented work — and the finding that prose-first held everywhere is
itself confirmation the birth-gate laws did their job.

## What could break, and what catches it

A new skill without an audit row → the set-equality arm. A pasted transcript in any tracked
skill → the ratchet, proven able to fire by a planted block. Versioned data fences stay
deliberately legal — they are the house pattern, not rot.
