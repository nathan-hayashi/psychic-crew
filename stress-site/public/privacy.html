What we do with your details

We are two people with a key board and a notebook. This page says exactly what is in the notebook, how long it stays there, and how to make it go away. It is written in the same plain words we would use at your kitchen table.

This page is a plain file. It loads one stylesheet and one script, both of them from this same site, and it asks for nothing from anywhere else. There is no tracker, no embedded map, no fetched typeface, and no measurement script — not as a policy we promise to keep, but as a fact you can confirm by reading the page source.

Collection — what we write down

We keep the smallest set of details that lets us turn up and do the job:

  • Your name, your street, and one phone number that reaches a person.
  • Your animal's name, age, and anything a stranger in your kitchen needs to know.
  • Your vet's practice name and number.
  • One emergency contact who is not you.
  • The visit notes: what was eaten, where we walked, what looked different.

Keys are recorded by a code on the board, never by your address, and the sheet that maps a code to a house lives in a locked drawer rather than in the notebook we carry.

We do not ask for your date of birth, your bank details, a copy of any document, or a spare key to anything except the door we have agreed to open.

Retention — how long we keep it

  • Visit notes: twelve months, then shredded.
  • Medication records: two years, because a vet may need to ask what was given and when.
  • Your contact details: for as long as you are a customer, and three months after the last visit in case you come back for a holiday week.
  • Keys and key codes: returned in person and struck off the board on the day you stop, not at the end of some later month.

Nothing on this list is kept "in case it is useful". When the period is up the paper is destroyed, and if you would rather have the notes back than see them shredded, say so and we will hand them over instead.

Contact — changing or destroying your details

Write to hello@kettlelane.example, or knock at number 14 — the green door with the boot scraper. Ask to see what we hold and we will read the whole entry back to you; ask us to destroy it and we will, then tell you it is done.

We answer once in the morning and once in the evening. If something has gone wrong and it cannot wait, phone rather than write.
